# Northwind-dataset
Northwind dataset
1) Revenue concentration in top products
A small group of top-selling products contributes a disproportionately large share of total revenue. This indicates that sales are driven mainly by a few bestsellers, so focusing inventory planning, promotions, and supplier negotiations on these high-performing products can significantly improve overall profitability.
2) Category performance varies significantly
Revenue is not evenly distributed across categories. Certain categories (like Beverages/Confections in Northwind) generate much higher sales compared to others. This suggests the business should prioritize marketing and assortment expansion in high-performing categories while reviewing pricing or promotion strategies for low-performing ones.
3) Presence of inactive customers
There are customers who have not placed any orders in the observed period. These inactive customers represent a potential growth opportunity through re-engagement campaigns such as targeted discounts, personalized emails, or loyalty programs to convert them into active buyers.
